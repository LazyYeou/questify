# Questify Gamification Ideas

This document outlines planned and proposed gamification systems for Questify to increase user engagement and provide a sense of progression.

## 1. Quest Tier System (Rarity)
Tasks are categorized into tiers that determine their visual style and reward multipliers.
*   **Common**: Basic tasks. Standard look, 1x EXP.
*   **Rare**: Important tasks. Blue accents, 1.5x EXP.
*   **Epic**: High-stakes tasks. Purple accents, 2x EXP.
*   **Legendary**: Massive undertakings (60+ min). Gold accents, 3x EXP.

## 2. Attribute Mapping (RPG Stats)
Connect task tags to specific character attributes to visualize personal growth.
*   **Intelligence**: Gained from tags like *Study, Reading, Coding*.
*   **Strength**: Gained from tags like *Gym, Workout, Health*.
*   **Focus**: Gained from tags like *Deep Work, Project, Writing*.
*   **Charisma**: Gained from tags like *Meeting, Networking, Social*.

## 3. Combo Multipliers (Consistency)
Reward daily consistency with EXP multipliers.
*   **3-Day Streak**: 1.1x EXP multiplier.
*   **7-Day Streak**: 1.3x EXP multiplier.
*   **14-Day Streak**: 1.5x EXP multiplier.
*   *Note: Missing a day resets the streak.*

## 4. Milestone Achievements
Specific "Titles" unlocked by unique behaviors.
*   **Early Bird**: Complete a quest before 8:00 AM.
*   **Night Owl**: Complete a quest after 11:00 PM.
*   **Deep Diver**: Complete a 90+ minute Legendary quest.
*   **Sprint Master**: Complete 5 tasks in a single day.
